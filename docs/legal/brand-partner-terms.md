# Brand Partner Terms

Version 0.2 · Effective date: [to be set at signature] · Between Tulbure Aron PFA ("DrinkMe") and the brand named in the partner form ("Partner").

These terms apply when the official DrinkMe account publishes content created with or about a Partner's drinks (for example recipes). The official account is run by DrinkMe and shown with an "Official" badge; the badge identifies DrinkMe as the publisher and is **not** the commercial-communication label, which every Partner post must also carry (see below). Posts on the official account are not hidden automatically by user reports, but DrinkMe's moderators review reports about them under the same rules as any other content and may remove them.

## 1. The collaboration
- Free of charge unless a separate written order sets a fee. Each publication is agreed by email (the "Brief": product, recipe, photos, publication date, markets).
- Nothing here creates exclusivity, a partnership or employment relationship.

## 2. Licence from the Partner
The Partner grants DrinkMe a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence, for the duration of the collaboration and for as long as the content stays on DrinkMe, to use the Partner's name, logos, product images, recipes and texts supplied for the Brief, to publish them in the DrinkMe app and DrinkMe's own channels. The Partner confirms it owns or is authorised to license them, and that they do not infringe third-party rights.

## 3. Approval
DrinkMe sends the final post to the Partner before publication. Nothing is published without the Partner's written approval (email is enough). DrinkMe keeps editorial control and may refuse content that conflicts with the Community Guidelines or law.

## 4. Disclosure
Every Partner post is clearly identified as a commercial communication, with the Partner's name, for example "Paid partnership with [Brand]" or "Collaboration with [Brand]" (for free collaborations involving gifted products) – in the app's language. (Directive 2005/29/EC Annex I point 11 and Romanian Law 363/2007; e-Commerce Directive 2000/31/EC Art. 6 / Romanian Law 365/2002; US FTC Endorsement Guides 16 CFR Part 255 – VERIFY.) **The label must exist in the app before the first Partner post (LC-11).**

## 5. Alcohol advertising rules
For alcoholic products, Partner content must:
- target and show only adults over the legal drinking age, with models who look at least 25 (industry codes – VERIFY);
- never be addressed to minors or show them drinking (Romanian Law 148/2000 – VERIFY the current article, reported as Art. 13 in the 2025 consolidation);
- never suggest that alcohol improves physical performance, social or sexual success, has therapeutic effects, or that high alcohol content is a quality;
- never encourage excessive drinking, or link alcohol with driving;
- include a responsible-drinking message where required by the market;
- respect market-specific rules: France (Loi Évin), Norway, Finland, Lithuania, Sweden and others restrict or ban alcohol advertising – the Brief lists the target markets and DrinkMe may limit visibility or decline (decision D-12);
- US: comply with the Distilled Spirits Council, Beer Institute and Wine Institute codes (e.g. adult audience share) – VERIFY.

## 6. Takedown
The Partner can ask DrinkMe to remove its content at any time; DrinkMe removes it within 5 business days (sooner if legally required). DrinkMe may remove Partner content at any time for legal or policy reasons.

## 7. Data
The parties exchange business contact details only. Each is a separate controller of them. DrinkMe does not share user personal data with Partners. Any aggregated statistics shared are anonymous.

## 8. Liability
Each party is liable for its own content and breaches. The Partner indemnifies DrinkMe against third-party claims arising from materials the Partner supplied. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 9. Term and law
These terms apply until either party ends them by email with 15 days' notice; sections 2 (for published content until takedown), 6, 7 and 8 survive. Romanian law; courts of Bucharest.
