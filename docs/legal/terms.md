## 7a. AI features (drink scanner and AI recipes)
**What they are for.** The **drink scanner** recognises a drink ingredient from a photo of its label (bottle, can, carton) or of a fresh ingredient, or from its barcode, so you can add it to your Smart Bar. **AI recipes** suggest drink ideas built around the item you scanned, or using only what is in your Smart Bar plus a few basics you choose. Recipes appear one by one as they are created. Both features are optional, free and for your personal, non-commercial use. They are available only to signed-in users who confirmed they are 18 or older (and, for alcoholic recipes, at least the legal drinking age where they live – §3) and who have read the in-app AI notice.

**How they work.** The AI runs on DrinkMe's own AI server, with open-weights models that we operate ourselves; when the first model is unsure about a label, a second, larger model on the same server reads it again. No outside AI company receives your photos or ingredients. If you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is looked up in Open Food Facts. Your photo is used only to answer you and is not kept. Details: Privacy Policy §5a and "How AI works in DrinkMe" (AI Transparency Notice).

**No guarantee of accuracy.** AI can be wrong. The scanner can misread a label, confuse products or get the alcohol content or volume wrong – check the result and correct it before you save it. Recipes are automatically generated suggestions, not tested recipes: they can contain mistakes in ingredients, amounts or steps. The strength shown (% alcohol and standard drinks) is an estimate. Use your own judgement.

**Allergies and safety.** The AI does not know your allergies or intolerances. Check every ingredient, and the labels of the products you use, yourself. Pay attention to the warnings we add (for example raw egg, or energy drinks mixed with alcohol, which we do not recommend).

**Drink responsibly – 18+ only.** Alcoholic recipes are only for adults of legal drinking age. Never drink and drive, never pressure anyone to drink, and skip alcohol if you are pregnant, take medication or are under the legal drinking age. Our safety rules remove recipes that are too strong or that encourage excessive drinking, but you remain responsible for your own consumption.
