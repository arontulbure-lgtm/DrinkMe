# Delete your DrinkMe account

Version 0.4 · Public page for the App Store / Google Play "delete account" link · Developer: Tulbure Aron PFA (DrinkMe)

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open DrinkMe and go to **Profile → Edit Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE** (or **STERGE** if the app is in Romanian) to confirm and sign in again when asked (for security we need a sign-in from the last few minutes; password users enter their password).
4. Your account is deleted. If removing your sign-in account is still finishing, the app tells you and we retry automatically.
You can also delete your account from the age-confirmation screen, without confirming your age first.

## Without the app
Write to **drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com** from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm it is you and delete the account within one month (usually much sooner).

## What is deleted
Your profile (name, city, bio, photo), posts and their photos, comments, cheers, saved posts, drink partners and requests, blocks, notifications, Smart Bar, the record of your age confirmation and of your privacy-notice and Terms acceptance, your entries in our security log, and your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication). If you used the AI features (once they are switched on), also your saved AI recipes, AI settings and your reports about AI answers. Scan photos are never kept, so there are none to delete.

## What is kept, and for how long
- **Backups:** your data is removed from all our backups within **30 days** (scheduled copies are kept at most 28 days; database history copies at most 28 days; our providers' backups are set to 30 days or less).
- **Reports you filed** that are still open stay until a moderator reviews them, **without your name or details**.
- **Moderation records about your account** in our security log are kept under an anonymous id for up to about 90 days.
- For about one hour we keep your user id only, so that an old sign-in cannot recreate the account.
- We cannot delete copies outside our control: screenshots others took, text other users wrote about you, or your Google profile photo (managed by Google).
