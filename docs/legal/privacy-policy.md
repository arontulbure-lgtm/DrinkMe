## 4. Who can see your data
- **Nobody without a DrinkMe account** can see anything on DrinkMe.
- **Signed-in users who confirmed their age** can see your name, photo, city and bio, your posts with their places, and your comments – once you have accepted the privacy notice. New accounts look blank to others until then.
- **Post owners** see who cheered or commented on their posts; cheer counts are visible to everyone who can see the post.
- **Your drink partner list** is visible only to you and your partners; your partners' other partners can see that you are partnered with them.
- **Private:** your email, saved posts, Smart Bar, blocks and the reports you send.
- **Blocking** hides each of you from the other.
- **Moderators** (people we appoint) can see reported content, who reported it, and the version of a post as it was when reported.
- Content reported by several people is hidden from others until a moderator reviews it.

- **Google Maps Platform** – when you search for a place, your phone sends the search text directly to Google.
- **Google** – if your profile photo comes from your Google account, other users' phones load it from Google.
- **Apple** – Sign in with Apple on iPhone.
- **Hosting provider** – stores our server and database (to be named before launch; we will choose an EU location where possible).

We do not sell or rent your personal data and do not share it for advertising.
**AI features** run on DrinkMe's own AI service; no outside AI company receives your photos or ingredients (§5a).

## 5a. AI features: drink scanner and AI recipes
These features are optional. You can use DrinkMe fully without them, for example by adding drinks to your Smart Bar by hand. They are only for signed-in users who confirmed they are 18 or older, and you must read a short AI notice before the first use. More about how the AI works and its limits: AI Transparency Notice (`ai-transparency.md`).

**Scanner (photo).** When you photograph a bottle, can, carton, fruit or herb, your phone sends the photo to DrinkMe's server. Our own AI service reads it there with an open-weights AI model and text recognition (OCR) that we run ourselves – the photo is **not** sent to an outside AI company. The photo is processed in memory and discarded once the answer is ready; we do **not** store it or write it to our logs – unless you turn on "Help improve the scanner" (below). The scanner only recognises drink ingredients; it does not identify people. Please photograph the product, not people.

**Barcode.** If you scan a barcode instead, our server sends **only the barcode number** to Open Food Facts (a free, open food database run by a non-profit association in France) to look up the product. Your name, account or IP address are not sent. Answers are cached for up to 24 hours without any link to you.

**AI recipes.** To create recipes we use the item you confirmed after a scan, or the drink names in your Smart Bar, plus your list of always-allowed basics (for example ice, water, sugar, salt). Recipes are not stored unless you save them. To avoid repeating the same recipe, the server remembers the recipe names it showed you for up to 2 hours, in memory only. If you have not confirmed you are 18+, only non-alcoholic recipes are possible.

**Saved recipes and AI settings.** We keep the recipes you save (up to 200), your basics list, the version and date of the AI notice you accepted, and whether "Help improve the scanner" is on. Only you can see them.

**"Help improve the scanner" (off by default).** Only if you turn it on in AI settings do we keep the photos you scan, together with what the scanner recognised, for **up to 90 days**, to test and improve the scanner. They are never shown to other users, never sold and never shared for advertising. Only the DrinkMe team (and our hosting provider, as our processor) can access them. Turning the switch off **deletes** the photos already kept; deleting your account deletes them too. Photos you take on your phone may contain metadata such as the place where they were taken; until we remove it automatically, please turn off location tagging in your camera if you do not want it kept.

**Legal bases (EU/UK).** Scanner, barcode lookup, recipes, saved recipes and settings: performance of our contract with you – you ask for the feature (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Keeping scan photos: your **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it at any time with the same switch, and this does not affect what happened before. Rate limits and safety checks of AI answers: our legitimate interest in a safe, working service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

**No automated decisions about you.** The AI suggests ingredients and recipes; it does not decide anything about you (GDPR Art. 22 does not apply).

**AI-generated content.** Every recipe and every photo scan result is labelled "AI-generated" in the app, and recipes carry an AI marker in the data we send to the app (EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 50).

**Other AI providers.** We do not send your photos or ingredients to OpenAI or any other outside AI company. If we ever do, we will update this policy, name the provider and ask for your permission in the app before it happens.

## 6. International transfers
Google and some providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. We rely on the safeguards described in your regional addendum (for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses).

## 7. How long we keep data
- Account and content: until you delete them or your account.
- Notifications: 7 days. Pending partner requests: 90 days. Abuse-limit counters: 24 hours.
- Report details: until a moderator resolves the report; the reporter's id is removed 30 days after it is resolved. Open reports you filed stay, without your name or details, after you delete your account, until reviewed.
- Suspension history and session-security values: as long as the account exists.
- Security log: about 90 days. After account deletion, moderation entries about you are kept only under an anonymous id.
- AI scan photos: not stored. With "Help improve the scanner" on: up to 90 days, deleted earlier when you turn it off or delete your account.
- Saved AI recipes, AI basics and AI notice acceptance: until you remove them or delete your account. Recipe names shown to you: 2 hours, in memory only.
- Backups: a deleted account is removed from our backups within 30 days.
- Server request logs: [period to be set with the hosting provider, at most 30 days – LC-15].

## 8. Deleting your account
In the app: Edit Profile → Delete account (also available on the age-confirmation screen). This deletes your profile, posts, comments, cheers, saved posts, partners, blocks, notifications, Smart Bar, saved AI recipes, AI settings, any scan photos kept with "Help improve the scanner" and your sign-in account, and removes your entries from the security log. What cannot be deleted at once: backups (gone within 30 days); copies others may have taken (for example screenshots); text other users wrote about you; and photos hosted by Google. Details: `account-deletion.md`.

## 9. Your choices and rights
You can view and edit your profile and posts in the app, delete content or your account, and block people. You can remove saved AI recipes and turn "Help improve the scanner" on or off at any time in AI settings. You can ask us for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, or to object, by writing to drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com. We answer within one month. Region-specific rights are in the addenda.

## 10. Children
DrinkMe is not for anyone under 18 or under the legal drinking age in their country. If you believe a minor uses DrinkMe, report the profile or write to us; we will delete the account.

## 11. Security
Encrypted connections (HTTPS) are required at launch; sign-in tokens are verified on the server; after a password reset or change, or if your sign-in account is disabled or removed, your older DrinkMe sessions stop working from your next sign-in (so after a reset, sign in again straight away); if the sign-in check with Google is not possible, we refuse the sign-in rather than skip the check; access to data follows strict rules; moderators see only what they need; backups are limited to 30 days. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell you and the authorities about a breach where the law requires.

## 12. Data on your phone
See the Device Storage Notice: we store your sign-in session and language on your phone; no tracking.

## 13. Changes
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app and ask you to read the updated notice before continuing.
