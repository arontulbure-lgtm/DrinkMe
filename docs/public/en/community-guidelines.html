DrinkMe Community Guidelines

Version 0.3 · Effective date: [to be set at publication]

DrinkMe is a place to discover and share great drinks – with or without alcohol. Keep it friendly, honest and safe. Breaking these rules can lead to content removal and suspension (see the Content Moderation Policy).

1. Adults only, responsibly

  • Only adults of legal drinking age in their country may use DrinkMe. Never post minors with alcohol, and never post content aimed at minors.
  • No pressure to drink. No drinking games, challenges, "how drunk" content, speed or quantity drinking, or mocking people who don't drink.
  • Never drink and drive – no content showing or encouraging drinking while driving, boating, at work where it is dangerous, or while pregnant.
  • No selling. Do not sell, trade or advertise alcohol, drugs or other regulated goods, and do not post prices or links for sales.
  • Be accurate. Mark alcoholic drinks as alcoholic and choose the right category. Non-alcoholic drinks are welcome and equal.
  • No content linking alcohol to sexual, social, sporting or professional success.

2. Respect people

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or content attacking people for who they are.
  • No sharing other people's private information or photos without permission.
  • No sexual content, nudity, or violent or shocking images.

3. Legal content only

  • Nothing illegal: no illegal drugs, weapons, fraud, scams, terrorism, or content that exploits anyone.
  • Respect intellectual property: post your own photos and recipes, or ones you have permission to use. See the Copyright Policy.

4. Be real

  • No impersonation. Do not pretend to be another person, a brand or DrinkMe. Only the official DrinkMe account may use the DrinkMe name or logo.
  • The "Official" badge next to a name means the account is run by DrinkMe itself (the operator named in our Imprint). No other account can get it. Do not imitate it, for example by adding "Official", a check mark or the DrinkMe logo to your name or profile photo to look like DrinkMe.
  • Reserved names. Profile names that contain "DrinkMe" are reserved for the official account, and the app will not save them for anyone else. This includes the name written with spaces, punctuation, accents, look-alike letters, digits or symbols in place of some letters (for example "Drink Me", "Dr1nkMe", "Drink★e") and names in which it appears inside a longer word (for example "Drink Meister"). So that no one can imitate the badge, the app also refuses names that contain the word "Official" or "Oficial" (also inside a longer word, for example "Unofficial") or a check mark (such as ✓ or ✅). If the name from your Google or Apple account is refused under these rules, we do not use it, we tell you why and ask you to choose a name. Names saved before this rule are not changed automatically, but the rule still applies to them: please change yours, and moderators can clear such a name after a report.
  • No spam, fake accounts, fake engagement, or misleading posts.
  • If a brand gave you something or paid you, say so clearly in your post (for example "#ad" or "gifted by …").

5. Use the tools

  • Report posts, comments or profiles that break these rules (reasons: spam, harassment, underage, dangerous drinking, mislabelled alcohol, other).
  • Block anyone you don't want to interact with.
  • Content reported by several people is hidden until a moderator checks it. The official DrinkMe account is the one exception: its content stays visible while a moderator reviews the reports, which are handled under the same rules (see the Content Moderation Policy). Don't misuse reports – false or coordinated reporting is itself a breach.
  • Illegal content, or content that infringes your rights, can also be reported by email to drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com, even if you don't have an account.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (112 in the EU) first.
