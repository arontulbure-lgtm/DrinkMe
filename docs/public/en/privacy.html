4. Who can see your data

  • Nobody without a DrinkMe account can see anything on DrinkMe.
  • Signed-in users who confirmed their age can see your name, photo, city and bio, your posts with their places, and your comments – once you have accepted the privacy notice. New accounts look blank to others until then.
  • Post owners see who cheered or commented on their posts; cheer counts are visible to everyone who can see the post.
  • Your drink partner list is visible only to you and your partners; your partners' other partners can see that you are partnered with them.
  • Private: your email, saved posts, Smart Bar, blocks and the reports you send.
  • Blocking hides each of you from the other.
  • Moderators (people we appoint) can see reported content, who reported it, and the version of a post as it was when reported.
  • Content reported by several people is hidden from others until a moderator reviews it.

5. Service providers and other recipients

  • Google (Firebase Authentication) – sign-in, sending verification and password-reset emails, and a check at every sign-in that your sign-in account is still active and whether your sessions were ended (for example by a password reset).
  • Google Maps Platform – when you search for a place, your phone sends the search text directly to Google.
  • Google – if your profile photo comes from your Google account, other users' phones load it from Google.
  • Apple – Sign in with Apple on iPhone.
  • Hosting provider – runs our server and stores the database and backups (to be named before launch; we will choose an EU location where possible).
  • Database provider – if we keep the database in a managed Postgres service instead of on the hosting provider's disk, that provider stores the database and its backups for us (to be named before launch; EU location where possible). Hosting and database providers act only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement.
  • Apple (App Attest / DeviceCheck) and Google (Play Integrity, Firebase App Check) – once we switch app-integrity checks on, your phone asks Apple or Google to confirm that it runs the genuine DrinkMe app and sends our server a short-lived token with each request. The token identifies the app, not you; our server checks it with Google and keeps only counts of valid and invalid tokens. Apple and Google process device information for this check under their own terms.
  • Email provider for our contact address.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal data and do not share it for advertising.

AI features (drink scanner, AI recipes) are switched off in this version. If we switch them on, §5a applies: they run on DrinkMe's own AI service, and no outside AI company receives your photos or ingredients.

5a. AI features: drink scanner and AI recipes (applies once the AI features are switched on)

This section applies only once the AI features are switched on in the app. Until then, nothing described here is collected.

These features are optional: you can use DrinkMe fully without them (for example by adding drinks to your Smart Bar by hand). They are only for signed-in users who confirmed they are 18 or older, and you must read and accept a short AI notice in the app before the first use.

Scanner (photo). When you photograph a bottle, can, carton, fruit or herb, your phone sends the photo to DrinkMe's server. Our own AI service reads it there with an open-weights AI model and text recognition (OCR) that we run ourselves on our own or rented servers – the photo is not sent to an outside AI company. The photo is processed in memory and discarded once the answer is ready; we do not store it or write it to our logs – unless you turn on "Help improve the scanner" (below). The scanner only recognises drink ingredients; it does not identify people. Please photograph the product, not people.

Barcode. If you scan a barcode instead, our server sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (a free, open food database run by a non-profit association in France). Your name, account and IP address are not sent. Answers are cached for up to 24 hours without any link to you. Barcode lookup is not AI.

AI recipes. To create recipes we use the item you confirmed after a scan, or the drink names in your Smart Bar, plus your list of always-allowed basics (for example ice, water, sugar, salt). Recipes are not stored unless you save them. To avoid repeating the same recipe, the server remembers the recipe names it showed you for up to 2 hours, in memory only.

Saved recipes and AI settings. We keep the recipes you save (up to 200), your basics list, the version and date of the AI notice you accepted, and whether "Help improve the scanner" is on (with the date you turned it on and the version of the text you agreed to). Only you can see them.

Reports about AI answers. If you report a recipe or a scan result in the app, we record the reason, the recipe id and name or the scan category, your user id and the time in our security log (about 90 days), to fix problems and improve our safety rules. No photo is included.

"Help improve the scanner" (off by default). Only if you turn it on in AI settings do we keep the photos you scan, together with what the scanner recognised, for up to 90 days, only to test and improve how well the scanner recognises drinks. We do not use your photos or other data to train AI models. Location and camera details stored in the photo file are removed before it is kept. The photos are never shown to other users, never sold and never used for advertising; only the DrinkMe team (and our hosting provider, as our processor) can access them. Turning the switch off deletes the photos already kept; deleting your account deletes them too.

Where. The AI service runs on a server with a graphics card rented from a hosting provider that acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. We will name the provider and its location here before the features are switched on, and we choose an EU location [provider and location to be added – decision D-23].

Legal bases (EU/UK). Scanner, barcode lookup, recipes, saved recipes, settings and the AI notice record: performance of our contract with you – you ask for the feature (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Keeping scan photos: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it at any time with the same switch, without affecting what happened before. Rate limits, safety checks of AI answers and reports about AI answers: our legitimate interest in a safe, working service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

No automated decisions about you. The AI suggests ingredients and recipes; it does not decide anything about you (GDPR Art. 22 does not apply).

AI-generated content. Every recipe and every photo scan result is labelled "AI-generated" in the app, and recipes carry an AI marker in the data we send to the app (EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 50).

Other AI providers. We do not send your photos or ingredients to OpenAI or any other outside AI company. If we ever do, we will update this policy, name the provider and ask for your permission in the app before it happens.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and content: until you delete them or your account.
  • Notifications: 7 days. Pending partner requests: 90 days. Abuse-limit counters: 24 hours.
  • Report details: until a moderator resolves the report; the reporter's id is removed 30 days after it is resolved. Open reports you filed stay, without your name or details, after you delete your account, until reviewed.
  • Suspension history and session-security values: as long as the account exists.
  • Security log (including Terms and privacy acceptance entries): about 90 days. After account deletion, moderation entries about you are kept only under an anonymous id.
  • Backups: a deleted account is removed from all our backups within 30 days (daily copies 7 days, weekly copies 28 days; if the database is in Postgres, its internal history copies at most 28 days, and the provider's own backups are set to 30 days or less).
  • Server request logs (no IP address): kept by the hosting provider for at most 30 days [exact period to be set with the hosting provider – LC-15].
  • IP addresses for abuse limits: up to 15 minutes, in memory only.
  • When the AI features are on (§5a): scan photos are not stored; with "Help improve the scanner" on, up to 90 days, deleted earlier when you turn it off or delete your account. Saved AI recipes, AI basics and AI notice acceptance: until you remove them or delete your account. Recipe names shown to you: 2 hours, in memory only.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Edit Profile → Delete account (also available on the age-confirmation screen). This deletes your profile, posts, comments, cheers, saved posts, partners, blocks, notifications, Smart Bar, (when the AI features are on) saved AI recipes, AI settings and any scan photos kept with "Help improve the scanner", and your sign-in account, and removes your entries from the security log. What cannot be deleted at once: backups (gone within 30 days); copies others may have taken (for example screenshots); text other users wrote about you; and photos hosted by Google. Details: Delete your DrinkMe account.

9. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile and posts in the app, delete content or your account, and block people. When the AI features are on, you can also remove saved AI recipes and turn "Help improve the scanner" on or off at any time in AI settings. You can ask us for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, or to object, by writing to drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com. We answer within one month. Region-specific rights are in the addenda.

10. Children

DrinkMe is not for anyone under 18 or under the legal drinking age in their country. If you believe a minor uses DrinkMe, report the profile or write to us; we will delete the account.

11. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS) are required at launch, including to the database; once switched on, app-integrity checks (§5) let our server refuse requests that do not come from the genuine app; sign-in tokens are verified on the server; after a password reset or change, or if your sign-in account is disabled or removed, your older DrinkMe sessions stop working from your next sign-in (so after a reset, sign in again straight away); if the sign-in check with Google is not possible, we refuse the sign-in rather than skip the check; access to data follows strict rules; moderators see only what they need; backups are limited to 30 days. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell you and the authorities about a breach where the law requires.

12. Data on your phone

See the Device Storage Notice: we store your sign-in session and language on your phone; no tracking.

13. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app and ask you to read the updated notice before continuing. Before anyone uses the AI features, the app asks them to accept a separate AI notice.
