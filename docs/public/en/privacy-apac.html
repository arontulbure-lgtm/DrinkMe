Japan (APPI) – minimum age 20

  • Purposes of use: Privacy Policy §3. We do not provide personal data to third parties except service providers we entrust (Firebase, hosting) and as required by law.
  • Foreign transfers: data is handled in Romania (EU) and by Google in the United States. Information on these countries' data protection systems: the EU has the GDPR (Japan recognises the EU under its adequacy framework); the US has no comprehensive federal privacy law. Google's protective measures: VERIFY. (APPI Art. 28 – VERIFY current numbering.)
  • Requests for disclosure, correction, suspension of use: drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com.
  • Security control measures: Privacy Policy §11.

South Korea (PIPA) – minimum age 19

  • Items collected, purposes, retention: Privacy Policy §§2, 3, 7.
  • Overseas transfer: to Google LLC (US) for authentication and place search, and to our hosting provider, at the time of sign-in/use, by network, for the retention periods in §7; necessary to perform our contract with you, which PIPA allows on the basis of disclosure in this policy (2023 amendment – VERIFY), and you may refuse by not using the service.
  • Destruction: data is deleted at the end of the retention period by deleting the records; backups expire within 30 days.
  • Privacy officer: Tulbure Aron, drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com. Domestic agent: not required below the thresholds (VERIFY).
  • Remedies: Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee, KISA privacy centre (privacy.kisa.or.kr).

Singapore (PDPA) – minimum age 18

We rely on your consent by use and on contractual necessity; you can withdraw consent by deleting your account. Data Protection Officer contact: drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com. Transfers are protected by comparable standards (Transfer Limitation Obligation).

Australia (Privacy Act 1988, APPs) – minimum age 18

We probably fall under the small business exemption (annual turnover up to AUD 3 million – VERIFY) but follow the APPs as good practice. Overseas recipients: EU (hosting), US (Google). Complaints: us first, then the OAIC (oaic.gov.au). Note: the Social Media Minimum Age law (16+) is met by our 18+ rule (VERIFY whether DrinkMe is an "age-restricted social media platform" and what "reasonable steps" require).

India (DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025) – excluded at launch (proposal)

The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 with most obligations applying from May 2027 (Mondaq, AZB, 2025). If India is offered later: consent notice in clear language, grievance officer, Data Protection Board complaints, and the 21/25 age question by state.

Thailand (PDPA) – minimum age 20

Lawful bases: contract and legitimate interest; rights to access, portability, objection, erasure, complaint to the PDPC. Transfers to the EU/US with safeguards.

Vietnam – minimum age 18

Vietnam's framework was Decree 13/2023/ND-CP; a Personal Data Protection Law adopted in 2025 is reported to apply from 1 January 2026 (not confirmed by our search on 2026-11-12 – VERIFY). Cross-border transfer impact assessments may be required. Decision D-02 whether to offer in Vietnam.

Mainland China (PIPL) – not offered (proposal, decision D-04)
