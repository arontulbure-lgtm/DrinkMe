Copyright and Intellectual Property Policy

Version 0.1 · Effective date: [to be set at publication]

We respect intellectual property and expect users to do the same. Only post photos, recipes and text you created or have permission to use.

1. Reporting infringement (all countries)

Send a notice to drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com with the subject "IP notice", including:

  1. Your name, address, telephone and email (or those of the person you represent);
  2. The work you say is infringed (e.g. your photo or recipe, a trademark registration number);
  3. Where the infringing content is on DrinkMe (user name, post description and date, screenshot);
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law;
  5. A statement that the information is accurate and, where applicable, that you are authorised to act for the rights holder;
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We review notices promptly, remove or disable access to infringing content, and tell the user who posted it (with your notice, from which we may remove your contact details unless the law requires them).

2. United States – DMCA (17 U.S.C. §512)

  • Designated agent: [TO BE REGISTERED with the U.S. Copyright Office – decision D-05]. Until registration, the safe harbour of §512(c) may not be available.
  • A notice under §512(c)(3) must contain the elements in section 1 above, plus a statement under penalty of perjury that you are authorised to act on behalf of the owner.
  • Counter-notice (§512(g)): if your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, send us: your name, address, phone; the removed content and where it was; a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake; consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if outside the US, any judicial district where we may be found) and to accept service from the complainant; your signature. We may restore the content in 10–14 business days unless the complainant tells us they have filed a court action.
  • Repeat infringers: accounts with repeated valid infringement notices are suspended permanently.

3. EU

We act expeditiously on notices under the Digital Services Act (Art. 16) and Directive 2001/29/EC. DrinkMe is not an "online content-sharing service provider" under Art. 17 of Directive (EU) 2019/790 at its current size and purpose – VERIFY.

4. Misuse

Knowingly false notices may make you liable for damages (e.g. 17 U.S.C. §512(f)).
