Privacy Policy – Addendum for the United States

Version 0.1 · Effective date: [to be set at publication] · Applies in addition to the DrinkMe Privacy Policy.

Minimum age in the US: 21. DrinkMe is not directed to children; we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (COPPA) or under 21.

1. California (CalOPPA, CCPA/CPRA) and other state laws

  • State comprehensive privacy laws (California CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon and others) apply above revenue or user-number thresholds that DrinkMe probably does not meet today (for example CCPA: annual revenue above the indexed threshold, or 100,000+ California consumers/households – VERIFY current figures). We nevertheless offer the rights below to every US user as good practice.
  • Categories collected (CCPA categories): identifiers (email, user id); personal records (name); internet activity on DrinkMe (posts, comments, cheers, saves, notifications); user-generated photos and text (audio/visual information); inferences: none; sensitive personal information: none intended (we do not collect precise geolocation). Sources, purposes and retention: Privacy Policy §§2, 3, 7.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of; we honour Global Privacy Control signals by default because we do not sell or share.
  • Your rights: to know/access, delete, correct, and to not be discriminated against for using them. Write to drinkmesocialapp@gmail.com; we verify by your account email and answer within 45 days. An authorised agent may act for you with written permission.
  • Do Not Track: we do not track you across sites or apps, so DNT signals change nothing.

2. Children (COPPA)

If you believe a child under 13 – or anyone under 21 – uses DrinkMe, write to us; we delete the account.

3. Content you post

Under Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act, users are responsible for their own content. Copyright notices: see the Copyright Policy (DMCA).

4. Data location

Data may be stored in the EU (our hosting) and processed by Google in the US.

5. Washington My Health My Data Act / Nevada / other health-data laws

We do not intend to collect consumer health data. Posts about alcohol are user content, not health tracking. VERIFY with counsel whether drink-consumption posts could be "consumer health data" under Washington's MHMDA before US launch.

Sources (checked 2026-11-12 from knowledge; VERIFY before US launch): California Business and Professions Code §22575–22579 (CalOPPA); Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq. (CCPA/CPRA) and its thresholds; 15 U.S.C. §§6501–6506 (COPPA); 47 U.S.C. §230; Washington RCW 19.373.
