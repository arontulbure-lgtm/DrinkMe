Age and Alcohol Policy

Version 0.1 · Effective date: [to be set at publication] · Operator: Tulbure Aron PFA ("DrinkMe", "we")

1. Who can use DrinkMe

DrinkMe is only for adults who have reached the legal age to buy alcohol in the country where they live, and never younger than 18. The minimum ages we apply are in section 2.

What the app does today: everyone must confirm "I am 18 or older" before any content is shown; nothing in DrinkMe is visible without signing in and making this confirmation. We do not ask for your birth date and we only store the date you confirmed. We are working on asking for your country of residence and applying the age for that country (see section 2). Until then, if the age in your country is higher than 18, you must not use DrinkMe until you reach it – this is a condition of our Terms.

If we learn that a user is under the required age, we close the account and delete its data.

4. Responsible drinking

  • DrinkMe celebrates taste, not quantity. Non-alcoholic drinks (mocktails, coffee, tea and others) are first-class.
  • We do not allow content that encourages excessive or fast drinking, drinking games or challenges, drinking and driving, drinking during pregnancy, or links alcohol with social, sexual or sporting success (see the Community Guidelines).
  • DrinkMe does not sell or deliver alcohol.
  • Need help? In Romania: talk to your doctor or contact the national health information line (VERIFY current number before publication). Elsewhere: your local health service.

5. Brand content

Posts by the official DrinkMe account that feature a brand are commercial communications. The official account is being set up now; we will not publish brand posts until they carry a clear "Paid partnership" or "Collaboration" label (LC-11). Brand posts are shown only to adults who passed the age check, never show or target minors, never encourage excessive drinking, and follow the alcohol advertising rules of each market. Details: Brand Partner Terms.
